Classical schedulers don't account for long-term thermal stress and hardware degradation. ThermoRL is a Lagrangian PPO-based reinforcement-learning agent that computes a Thermal Health Index (THI) from degradation-linked parameters and uses it to make placement decisions across air-cooled, direct-to-chip, and immersion cooling zones.
